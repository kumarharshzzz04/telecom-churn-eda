Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

A telecom company was losing customers without knowing who, when, or why. This analysis of 7,000+ customer records identified the exact segments driving churn — giving the business a clear, prioritized retention strategy.
What I found that actually matters:

- Month-to-month contract customers churn at dramatically higher rates — the single biggest lever for retention spend
- 80% of churn happens in the first few months — meaning onboarding is broken, not the product
- Fiber optic customers are paying more and leaving more — a pricing or service quality red flag
- Customers without TechSupport churn at nearly 2x the rate — one upsell conversation could change that

The business can now: stop spraying retention budget across all customers and target the top 3 high-risk segments specifically.
Stack: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn

Conclusion

This analysis demonstrates that customer churn is not random. It is strongly influenced by contract commitment, pricing, service type, and access to support services. Structured exploratory analysis was sufficient to uncover clear and actionable business insights.
